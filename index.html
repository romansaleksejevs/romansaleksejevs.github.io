About me

My name is Romans Aleksejevs, and I am a 37-year-old Senior Network & Security Engineer based in Lund, Sweden. I was born in Riga, Latvia, where I grew up and spent most of my life before moving to Sweden. I have been living and working in Sweden for more than 5 years.

I hold a Master’s Degree in Information Technology and have more than 18 years of professional experience in the IT industry. Throughout my career, I have worked with enterprise networks, mobile network operators, ISPs, data center infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud technologies, and network automation. I also hold multiple industry certifications, including Cisco, Microsoft Azure, and LPIC certifications.

I have extensive hands-on experience with network infrastructure and security technologies, including routers, switches, load balancers, firewalls, VPNs, and DDoS protection solutions. I have also worked with network automation and management platforms such as Cisco ISE, Cisco ACI, Tufin, and AlgoSec, as well as private and public cloud environments. For network automation projects, I have used Ansible, Python, GraphQL, and Terraform to automate infrastructure configuration, deployment, and operational tasks.

Currently, my main professional focus is Network Automation and Artificial Intelligence, exploring how automation and AI can improve network operations, reliability, efficiency, and security. I am continuously developing my technical skills and am open to new professional challenges and opportunities.
